# Key Decisions

**Function:** dated records of the business's key decisions, covering what was decided, the options considered, and why they were made. Not only decisions about the agents.

## Overview

This hub holds every decision worth a record, whether it is about how the business runs or how its agents work, so anyone asking why a call was made can find the reasoning in one place. A record is never edited after the fact. A reversal is a new decision, and the old one is marked superseded.
