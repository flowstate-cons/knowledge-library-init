# Research

**Function:** investigations and their findings, kept apart from settled pages until they are confirmed.

## Overview

Research answers "what did we find out, and how sure are we." Each page states the question, how it was investigated, what was found, and a confidence level, using the Research blueprint. Keeping unconfirmed findings here stops them being mistaken for fact.

A confirmed finding moves into the page it belongs on (Reference for a fact, a Standard or Pattern for a way of working, a Business page for anything about the company), with the owner's agreement. The research page stays as the record of how it was learned.
