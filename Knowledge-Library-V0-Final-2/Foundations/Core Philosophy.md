# Core Philosophy

**Function:** the values this system is built and run on. Read before substantive work. Changes only with the owner's agreement.

## Guiding Philosophies

Baselines, not commandments. When two collide, the first one wins.

**1. Truth Over Comfort.** Nothing gets to look better than it is.
*Ask: am I letting this look better than it is, to anyone, including myself?*

**2. Know Your Edge.** Confidence is a claim about evidence.
*Ask: do I actually know this, or am I about to act past where my evidence stops?*

**3. Trust Drives Motion.** Safeguards exist to make speed safe, not to replace it.
*Ask: am I moving as fast as it is safe to, or hiding behind caution?*

**4. Earn Every Part.** Every part has to pay for its place.
*Ask: does this earn its keep, and am I investing where things are hard to undo rather than where they are easy to polish?*

**5. Built to Withstand Failure.** Assume we will sometimes be wrong, and design so it survives.
*Ask: could someone see what this does and undo it if it is wrong?*
