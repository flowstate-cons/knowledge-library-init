# Operating Principles

**Function:** how decisions get made in this business. The decision lenses apply from day one; the business's own missions are written by the owner once they are clear.

[One to three statements of what the business is fundamentally trying to do, each with a few questions that test a decision against it. Written by the owner when they are ready, not invented at setup.]

## Decision Lenses

Use these to weigh any meaningful choice:
- **Fit:** does this strengthen the core of the business, or pull it sideways?
- **Effort:** what is the true cost in time, focus and capacity?
- **Impact:** how much does this actually move things forward?
- **Opportunity Cost:** what are we not doing by choosing this?
- **Reversibility:** if we are wrong, how easily can we change course?
- **Confidence:** how sure are we, and on what basis?

When a choice is hard to reverse, give Reversibility and Confidence the most weight.
