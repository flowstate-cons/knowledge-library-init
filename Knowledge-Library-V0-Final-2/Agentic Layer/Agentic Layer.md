# Agentic Layer

**Function:** the agents themselves: who they are, what they can do, the rules they work under, and what the system has learned.

## Overview

The agents working in this system and everything about how they operate. Each subfolder has a hub explaining what belongs in it.

## Scope

- [[Agents]]: one page per agent.
- [[Skills]]: what each skill does.
- [[Standards]]: rules agents must follow. If an agent can be in violation of it, it goes here.
- [[Patterns]]: proven methods an agent chooses to use.
- [[Reference]]: settled facts to look up.
- [[Research]]: investigations and findings not yet confirmed.
- [[Decisions]]: dated records of choices.
- [[Loops]]: recurring processes.

Not sure which? See What Goes Where in [[Glossary]].

## Limitations

- Business processes belong in Business, even when an agent runs them.
