# Standards

**Function:** the binding rules for how work is done. The test: if an agent can be in violation of it, it is a Standard.

## Overview

A Standard is a rule the agents must follow, such as "every client email is reviewed by the owner before it is sent" or "prices are only quoted from the current price sheet". Each rule is marked as a Law (no exceptions) or a Principle (expected, departure allowed with a stated reason). See [[Rule Tiers]].

Standards are earned, not declared. A rule usually starts life as a Candidate in Core Philosophy or as a Pattern that keeps proving itself, and is promoted here with the owner's agreement once it has.

## Scope

- One page per area of work (for example client communication or pricing), from the Standard blueprint, holding that area's Laws and Principles.
- Only rules the owner has agreed to.

## Limitations

- A method an agent chooses to use, and could reasonably skip, is a Pattern.
- A fact to look up is Reference. A finding not yet confirmed is Research.
- Behavior that applies to every agent in every task belongs in Foundations.
