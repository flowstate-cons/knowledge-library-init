# Research

**Function:** investigations and their findings, kept apart from settled pages until they are confirmed.

## Overview

Research answers "what did we find out, and how sure are we?" Every page states the question, how it was investigated, what was found, and a confidence level. Keeping unconfirmed findings here stops them being mistaken for fact.

A confirmed finding moves into the page it belongs on (Reference for a fact, a Standard or Pattern for a way of working, a Business page for anything about the company), with the owner's agreement. The research page stays as the record of how it was learned.

## Scope

- One page per investigation, from the Research blueprint: question, method, findings, confidence.

## Limitations

- Settled facts belong in Reference.
- Market or competitor research about the business itself can live in Business/Sales and Marketing once it is confirmed.
