**Title:** Dropping Saturday Delivery
**Function:** Records the decision to stop offering Saturday delivery. This allows anyone asking why delivery ends Friday to find the reasoning in one place.
**Decision:** Riverside Bakery stopped offering Saturday delivery starting in March.
**Context:** Saturday orders had grown too large for the one delivery driver to finish before close.
**Rationale:** Weekday delivery already covered nearly all demand, and adding a second driver was not worth the cost.
