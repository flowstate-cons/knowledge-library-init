# Profile

**Function:** capture the owner's role, strengths and watch-outs, along with how agents should adapt to each. This understanding allows agents to work to the owner's strengths and around their weak spots.

## Overview

This page holds the owner's role, background, strengths and watch-outs, with the adaptations agents make for each. The library manager builds it with the owner during Initialization, and either of them updates it as it becomes clearer.

## Identity

- The owner's name and role go here.
- What shaped how they work or decide, if anything worth naming, goes here.

## Strengths

[What the owner is genuinely strong at, so agents hand that work back to them rather than attempting it. Left empty until the owner names some.]

## Watch-Outs
