# Personal Overview

**Function:** give agents a working understanding of the owner, covering their role, preferences and voice. As the entry point to the personal pillar, it leads to everything an agent needs to work with the owner well.

## Overview

This is the owner's own pillar within the library. It holds who they are, how they like to work, and how they write, so agents can work with them well rather than starting from nothing each time.

## Contents

- [[Profile]] captures the owner's role, strengths and watch-outs.
- [[Preferences]] records the owner's personal choices in how work is done and written, and the decisions already settled.
- [[Voice and Style]] captures the owner's writing voice from real samples.

## Connections

- [[Business Overview]] holds the business the owner runs.
- [[Agentic Layer Overview]] holds the agents the owner works with.
